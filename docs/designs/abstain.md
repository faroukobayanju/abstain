## Problem Statement

Autonomous trading agents are easy to build and impossible to trust. Anyone can wire an
LLM to a signal feed and have it place orders. Nobody can show a third party *why* a
given order was allowed, what conditions would have stopped it, or that the track record
being advertised wasn't assembled after the fact.

The X-Agent trading track asks for an Agent/MCP application built around a validated
OlaXBT Nexus strategy. The official 100-point scorecard makes the opening explicit:

| Area | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Real agent/user value | 30 |
| **Demonstrated capability quality** — *"live results, error behavior, limits, evidence"* | **25** |
| Engineering and maintainability | 20 |
| MCP productization readiness | 15 |
| Adoption and operating potential | 10 |

A quarter of the score is error behavior and limits. Most entries will demo only the
happy path.

## What Makes This Cool

**The refusal is the demo.** `Abstain` takes a Nexus strategy signal and runs it through
ten deterministic pre-trade checks. It emits a verdict — `EXECUTE` or `ABSTAIN` — plus a
hash-chained receipt naming every check, its observed value, and its threshold. Every
decision, including every refusal, is replayable by a stranger.

The 60-second demo is an adversarial proof, the pattern the
[hackathon-idea-library](https://github.com/faroukobayanju/hackathon-idea-library) rubric
prizes most: *"a forbidden transaction visibly fails."*

1. Feed a clean signal → `EXECUTE`. It works.
2. Feed the same signal under a hostile funding regime → **`ABSTAIN: FUNDING_REGIME
   observed=+0.19%/8h threshold=+0.05%`**. Visible, specific, non-negotiable.
3. Hand the judge the receipt. They run `POST /v1/verify` and recompute the chain
   themselves. Tamper with one byte of history and verification reports the exact
   sequence number where the chain diverges.

Nothing here asks the judge to trust the builder.

strategy signal and execution**, and makes every refusal a replayable receipt. That join is
the differentiator.

## Premises

All five confirmed by the builder during the session:

1. Refusal is the product — 25 of 100 points are error behavior and limits, and that is
   where the field is weakest.
2. Nexus must be load-bearing. Remove it and there is no signal, no
   `QUALIFIED_FOR_OKX_LISTING` status, and no `as_of` series to gate against. Passes the
   removal test.
3. A deterministic gate beats an LLM judge. Reviewers can replay determinism;
   "the model decided" is not evidence. This also protects the reproducibility hard gate.
4. Two days means one capability done completely, not three done partially.
5. This is trading/settlement logic, not on-chain security — but the framing is load
   bearing for eligibility and must be policed in every document.

## The gate — ten deterministic checks

Each check is a pure function returning `{id, verdict, observed, threshold, unit}`.
Any `FAIL` ⇒ overall `ABSTAIN`. Three verdicts exist: `EXECUTE`, `ABSTAIN`, and
**`NO_TRADE`** — a `trade_intent: "HOLD"` signal short-circuits before gating, because
nothing was proposed. `NO_TRADE` still writes a receipt (empty `checks` array plus a stated
reason) so the chain has no gaps for `/v1/verify` to trip over. Checks always all run — a receipt shows every check,
not just the first failure, because the limits *are* the evidence.

| # | Check | Fails when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `SIGNAL_STALE` | signal timestamp older than `max_signal_age_s` |
| 2 | `NOT_QUALIFIED` | `get_strategy_metrics` status ≠ `QUALIFIED_FOR_OKX_LISTING` |
| 3 | `FUNDING_REGIME` | funding rate beyond threshold *and* against the signal's direction |
| 4 | `OI_SHOCK` | open-interest delta over the window exceeds `max_oi_delta_pct` |
| 5 | `DRAWDOWN_BUDGET` | equity drawdown from peak exceeds `max_drawdown_pct` |
| 6 | `CORRELATED_CLUSTER` | N same-direction entries share an entry bar across correlated majors (≥ `max_cluster_size`); single-symbol exposure over `max_symbol_pct` is the cheaper sub-condition |
| 7 | `LOSS_STREAK` | consecutive losing trades ≥ `max_loss_streak` (cool-down) |
| 8 | `DATA_GAP` | a required series is missing or incomplete for the window |
| 9 | `SIZE_BOUND` | notional outside `[min_notional, max_notional]` |
| 10 | `DUPLICATE` | this `signal_id` was already acted on (replay protection) |

Thresholds live in one versioned `policy.json`. The policy hash goes into every receipt,
so a reviewer can tell whether two decisions were made under the same rules.

## Receipts

```jsonc
{
  "seq": 41,
  "prev_hash": "sha256:…",
  "ts": "2026-09-18T14:02:11Z",
  "signal_id": "sig_…",
  "policy_hash": "sha256:…",
  "inputs_digest": "sha256:…",     // canonical digest of every Nexus response consulted
  "checks": [ { "id": "FUNDING_REGIME", "verdict": "FAIL", "observed": 0.0019,
                "threshold": 0.0005, "unit": "rate_per_8h" } ],
  "verdict": "ABSTAIN",
  "hash": "sha256:…"               // over the canonical form of everything above
}
```

`POST /v1/verify` recomputes the chain from genesis and returns `ok`, or the first
sequence number where the recomputed hash diverges. That endpoint is the demo's closer.

## API surface

Deliberately tiny — "MCP productization readiness" (15 pts) rewards crisp boundaries.

| Endpoint | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /v1/evaluate` | `{signal_id?, symbol, side, notional}` → verdict + all ten checks + receipt |
| `GET /v1/receipts?from=&to=` | List |
| `GET /v1/receipts/{seq}` | One |
| `POST /v1/verify` | Replay chain → `ok` or divergence seq |
| `GET /v1/policy` | Current thresholds + policy hash |
| `GET /health` | **Hard gate** — `{status: "ok", commit: "<40-char sha>"}` |
| `GET /.well-known/xagent-verification.json` | **Hard gate** — `{slug, commit}` |

Side effects: none. Abstain never places an order. It authorizes or refuses, and records
why. That keeps the MCP tool boundary honest and keeps the entry clear of custody.

## Approaches Considered

- **A. Gate + recorded evidence chart** — chosen.
- **B. Gate only, no chart** — rejected: weakest against the 30-point value box.
- **C. Full live three-stage** — rejected: `run_backtest` cannot sweep dates, so the live
  no-peek engine is now a real subsystem that loses to the clock.
- **D. Sealed Forecast** — rejected: weak on the 25-point error-behavior box, easiest to clone.

## Recommended Approach

**The deterministic gate is the live product; a with-gate vs without-gate equity chart
ships as committed evidence.**

### Revised 2026-09-17 after CEO review

`run_backtest` takes **only `n_bars` (30–120)** and replays the latest saved config. It
cannot sweep `as_of` dates. The original no-lookahead artifact would therefore have to be
built from scratch against the raw historical endpoints, and it would make a claim about
Nexus internals that cannot be verified from outside.

**Replaced with:** replay the same signal set twice, once through the gate and once
without, and chart both equity curves. It reuses `get_strategy_trades` and
`get_strategy_equity` (already called by the gate), makes no claim about anyone else's
engine, and it is a picture of *this product working* rather than a general critique of
backtesting.

### Stack

TypeScript + Hono on Vercel Functions (Node runtime). Gate is pure functions. Store is
pluggable: **Upstash Redis in production with an atomic Lua append**, file-backed with an
exclusive lock in dev and in the reviewer's isolated run. Vitest.

### Strategy configuration

**Deterministic mode, 1–2 desks.** Agentic mode runs an LLM per bar (~5 min for a 30-bar
test on 1–2 desks per the sponsor guide), costs credits, and makes the proposer
non-reproducible — which undercuts the reproducibility hard gate the whole submission
rests on. Deterministic makes the pipeline replayable end to end.

## Approved review decisions

| # | Finding | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Receipt chain forks under concurrent Vercel invocations | **Redis atomic Lua append.** Read-head, hash, write-head in one uninterruptible script. File store uses an exclusive lock for the same guarantee. |
| 2 | Fail-open vs fail-closed on Nexus failure undefined | **Fail closed.** Every Nexus error class resolves to `ABSTAIN` + `DATA_GAP`, with the error class named in the receipt. |
| 3 | `/health` dependency boundary undefined | **Zero dependencies.** `/health` returns `{status, commit}` only. `/v1/ready` reports Nexus/Redis for debugging and no gate reads it. |
| 4 | Deployed service and PR live in different repos | **Own public repo deployed and pinned; byte-identical vendored copy under `source/` in the fork's PR.** |
| 5 | `/v1/evaluate` is an unauthenticated write to the evidence chain | **Shared write key** (`X-ABSTAIN-KEY`, published in SUBMISSION.md as a demo credential). All read endpoints stay fully public. |

  └──────────┘   verdict + receipt   └──────┬───────┬──────┘

                        X-API-KEY: nxk_     │       │ EVAL <lua> (atomic)

## State machine — one evaluation

```
   RECEIVED ──auth ok──▶ FETCHING ──all series──▶ GATING ──▶ APPENDING ──▶ DONE
      │                     │                       │            │
      │ 401                 │ any Nexus error       │            │ store down
      ▼                     ▼                       ▼            ▼
   REJECTED            ABSTAIN(DATA_GAP)      ABSTAIN|EXECUTE   503
                             │                       │       (no receipt
                             └───────────────────────┘        written, no
                                   both append                 partial state)

  Impossible: DONE without an appended receipt. Prevented by appending before responding.
```

## Error & Rescue Registry

```
ERROR CLASS              | RESCUED? | ACTION                           | CALLER SEES
-------------------------|----------|----------------------------------|---------------------
NexusTimeoutError        | Y        | retry 2x backoff → fail closed   | ABSTAIN + DATA_GAP
NexusAuthError           | Y        | no retry, fail closed, log error | ABSTAIN + DATA_GAP
NexusRateLimitError      | Y        | backoff, retry 1x → fail closed  | ABSTAIN + DATA_GAP
NexusUpstreamError       | Y        | retry 1x → fail closed           | ABSTAIN + DATA_GAP
NexusParseError          | Y        | no retry, fail closed            | ABSTAIN + DATA_GAP
ChainForkError           | Y        | impossible by construction (Lua) | n/a
StoreUnavailableError    | Y        | no receipt written, named 503    | 503 + reason
AbstainAuthError         | Y        | 401, logged                      | 401 + reason
```

No catch-all handlers. Every branch names a class. Every receipt for a Nexus failure
carries the originating error class, so `NexusTimeoutError` and `NexusAuthError` stay
distinguishable in the evidence chain.

## Failure Modes Registry

```
CODEPATH            | FAILURE MODE        | RESCUED? | TEST? | USER SEES      | LOGGED?
--------------------|---------------------|----------|-------|----------------|--------
NexusClient#*       | 5 error classes     | Y        | Y     | ABSTAIN+DATA_GAP| Y
ReceiptStore#append | concurrent head     | Y (Lua)  | Y     | correct chain  | Y
ReceiptStore#append | store unreachable   | Y        | Y     | 503 + reason   | Y
GateEngine#evaluate | missing series      | Y        | Y     | ABSTAIN+DATA_GAP| Y
/v1/evaluate        | missing write key   | Y        | Y     | 401 + reason   | Y
/v1/verify          | tampered receipt    | Y        | Y     | divergence seq | Y
Studio config stale | MCP serves old cfg  | PARTIAL  | N     | wrong values   | Y (digest)
```

**Remaining known limit:** stale Studio config is silent at the Nexus boundary — there is
no staleness signal to read. Mitigated by recording `inputs_digest` in every receipt and
logging it, which makes it diagnosable after the fact. Stated honestly in SUBMISSION.md.

## Deployment & rollback

Order matters and doing it backwards costs a redo:

```
  freeze code ──▶ push ──▶ capture 40-char sha ──▶ deploy ──▶ verify /health echoes sha
                                                                      │
                                    ┌─────────────────────────────────┘
                                    ▼
                    vendor byte-identical copy into fork's source/
                                    ▼
                    write submission.json pinning that sha ──▶ open PR
```

**Rollback is not "revert to previous deployment"** — that changes the sha `/health`
reports and desynchronizes it from `submission.json`, failing a hard gate. Rollback means
**redeploy the pinned commit**.

### First backtest — run bt-7a0daf243c06, strategy str_515920d047ca (2026-09-17)

```
19 trades · Jun 23 → Sep 17 (~87 daily bars) · Exposure 100%
Sharpe 0.30 · Sortino 0.27 · Calmar 0.14 · total return +1.07% · ann. vol 19.68%
Max DD 7.6% · Avg DD -1.9% · VaR95 -1.9% · CVaR95 -2.5%
Win rate 52.6% · Profit factor 1.15 · Expectancy 2.09 · Kelly 0.0%
Consec. wins 3 · Consec. losses 3 · Avg hold 15.5 bars
```

**Open risk:** Sharpe 0.30 and Kelly 0.0% mean the strategy has close to no edge. If
`get_strategy_metrics` returns `NOT_QUALIFIED`, check #2 fails on every evaluation and
Abstain never returns `EXECUTE` — no happy path for the demo. **Verify before building
check #2:** `curl -s .../get_strategy_metrics -H "X-API-KEY: $NEXUS_API_KEY"`.
If `NOT_QUALIFIED`, make `require_qualified` a policy flag and demo two receipts under two
`policy_hash` values, which also demonstrates the policy-hash mechanism doing real work.

**Do not use these fields in any check or claim:** `Ulcer Index 0.00`, `Skewness 0.00`,
`Kurtosis 3.00`. Kurtosis of exactly 3.00 and skewness of exactly 0.00 on 19 trades are
implausible, and Ulcer Index 0.00 contradicts a 7.6% drawdown. They read as placeholders.

**Chart source:** the worst stretch is Jun 23 → Jul 2 (drawdown to about -8%). With

### Differentiation from Nexus's own Risk Gate (must appear in SUBMISSION.md)

The Nexus pipeline is `Market Scan → Arbitrator → **Risk Gate** → Portfolio Mgmt → Executor`.
A judge will ask why Abstain is not redundant. The answer, stated explicitly:

Nexus's Risk Gate is **internal, pre-signal, and invisible** — it enforces position and
leverage limits inside the strategy and halts on drawdown, producing no external artifact.
Abstain sits **outside**, after `get_strategy_signal`, and emits a hash-chained receipt a
stranger can replay. Nexus's gate has no receipt, no verify endpoint, and no way for a
third party to confirm a refusal occurred.

They are complementary by construction: Abstain's thresholds sit *inside* Nexus's, so
Abstain refuses and records evidence before Nexus silently halts.

## Check #2 resolution — `require_qualified` policy flag

Sharpe 0.30 against a published gate of 2.0 means `NOT_QUALIFIED` on every call, which
would leave Abstain with no `EXECUTE` path. Resolved by making check #2 configurable:

- `policy.strict.json` — `require_qualified: true` → `ABSTAIN`, check #2 FAIL, observed
  `sharpe_ratio=0.30` vs `threshold=2.0`.
- `policy.permissive.json` — `require_qualified: false` → check #2 SKIPPED, the other nine
  decide.

The demo becomes: the **same signal**, two published policies, two verdicts, two receipts
carrying different `policy_hash` values. That is what `policy_hash` is for, and it
demonstrates the mechanism rather than merely including it. Both receipts are honest; no
synthetic data anywhere.
